# WristMemo 1.3

TestFlight build: 10

Released: 2026-08-08

## What's new

- The Watch capture surface now uses clean Helvetica typography and a clearer
  **OFF / Tap to start recording** ready state. The green **MESSAGE RECEIVED /
  Launching background agent…** receipt remains visible for a more readable
  2.5 seconds, while the next memo remains ready to start immediately.
- Signing in on the iPhone is now the single setup step for secure automatic
  transcription. Safely delivered Watch recordings queue in the background,
  recover older deferred uploads, and process one at a time so a backlog cannot
  overwhelm delivery.
- Upload reliability is improved for interrupted background work and temporary
  service responses. The original audio remains safe on the devices until the
  next durable hand-off is confirmed.

## What to test

1. On Watch, confirm the grey **OFF** screen starts a memo and the red
   **RECORDING** screen stops it. Verify the Helvetica labels are legible.
2. Stop a memo and confirm the green **MESSAGE RECEIVED / Launching background
   agent…** receipt is visible long enough to read. Start another memo during
   the receipt; it must begin immediately.
3. On iPhone, sign in with Google once, make a Watch memo, and confirm it
   appears in Voice Memos and advances through background transcription without
   an additional approval prompt.
