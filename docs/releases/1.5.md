# WristMemo 1.5

TestFlight build: 12

Released: 2026-08-08

## What's new

- The iPhone app is now a quieter, content-first **Thoughts** library. Completed
  transcripts lead each row, routine delivery status stays out of the way, and
  setup and processing language is shorter.
- Search and original-audio playback remain available. A recording that needs
  help stays visible with a clear retry action.

## What to test

1. Open the iPhone app and confirm completed thoughts show the transcript first
   with only the capture date beneath it.
2. Capture a new Watch memo and confirm it appears as **Processing…**, then
   becomes a searchable transcript without extra upload controls or badges.
3. Open a thought and confirm the full transcript is selectable and the
   original recording plays while it is retained.
4. If a transcription is in a failed state, confirm **Needs attention** is
   visible and **Retry** safely requeues it.
