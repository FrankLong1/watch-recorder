# WristMemo 1.1

TestFlight build: 6

Released: 2026-08-08

## What's new

- A completed Watch capture now briefly shows a green **MESSAGE RECEIVED**
  receipt with “Launching background agent…” at the stop haptic. This is a
  deterministic local confirmation that the audio has been committed for
  delivery; it never waits for an agent or network request.
- The iPhone companion now provides a searchable voice-memo review library.
  Completed transcripts are stored in a protected local cache, synced from the
  authenticated owner-only history feed, and remain reviewable after the
  temporary local audio has been retained or removed.
- Ingest and transcript access now use authenticated owner identities, while
  the Watch capture path remains local-first and audio remains the source of
  truth until its durable hand-off completes.

## What to test

1. Start a memo from READY or the Action Button, speak, then stop it. Confirm
   the stop haptic and the short green receipt appear together; immediately
   starting another memo must still work.
2. On the phone, sign in and pull to refresh the Voice Memos library. Confirm
   completed notes can be searched, opened, and read as full transcripts.
3. If the recording is still retained locally, verify the phone playback button
   works; a completed transcript must remain visible after temporary audio
   cleanup.
