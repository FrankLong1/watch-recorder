# WristMemo 1.7

TestFlight build: 14

Released: 2026-08-09

## What's new

- Makes the assigned Apple Watch Ultra Action Button the only WristMemo start
  path: taps on **OFF** no longer begin a recording, and a second Action Button
  press leaves an active recording untouched.
- Refines the Swiss full-screen typography with **PRESS ACTION BUTTON** under
  **OFF** and **TAP TO STOP** under **RECORDING**.
- Removes the watch-face complication and Siri/Shortcuts start actions so start
  and stop always have separate meanings.

## What to test

1. From the watch face, press the assigned Action Button and confirm WristMemo
   opens directly into the red **RECORDING / TAP TO STOP** screen.
2. While **OFF**, tap anywhere on the screen and confirm recording does not
   start. Press the Action Button and confirm it does start.
3. While recording, press the Action Button again and confirm recording
   continues. Then tap the full screen or use Double Tap and confirm it stops
   and saves.
4. Start another memo, then exit WristMemo. Confirm the recording stops and the
   memo is delivered to the iPhone.
