# WristMemo 1.6

TestFlight build: 13

Released: 2026-08-09

## What's new

- Fixes the Apple Watch Ultra Action Button showing **“Your Agents failed”**
  instead of opening WristMemo and starting a recording.
- Keeps the Swiss-spaced full-screen **OFF** and **RECORDING** design from 1.4.

## What to test

1. Assign **Your Agents** as the Watch Action Button control, then press the
   Action Button from the watch face. Confirm WristMemo opens directly into the
   red **RECORDING** screen.
2. Speak immediately after the start haptic, then tap the full screen to stop.
   Confirm the memo is saved and reaches the iPhone.
3. Press the Action Button again from **OFF** and confirm a second recording
   starts without an error or extra choice screen.
